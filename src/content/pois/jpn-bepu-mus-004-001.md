    The Beppu City Bamboo Crafts Center is an exhibition facility showcasing the finest examples of Beppu bamboo crafts, designated as a National Traditional Craft. Here, you can not only admire the beautiful bamboo crafts but also delve into their historical background, the skilled techniques of the artisans, and the charm of bamboo itself.
    
    The history of Beppu bamboo crafts is long, with records indicating that bamboo baskets were already being made in the Nara period. During the Edo period, bamboo bath baskets and pillows were made for visitors to the Beppu hot springs, and the techniques developed in response to this demand. Since the Meiji period, the value of bamboo crafts has increased not only as practical items for daily use but also as fine art crafts, and the tradition and techniques have been passed down to the present day.
    
    Inside the center, you can learn about the history of bamboo crafts, the various types of bamboo used, and the techniques such as weaving and lacquering. The exhibited works range from everyday items such as baskets and sieves to delicate and artistic flower vases and objects, allowing you to fully appreciate the diverse expressions of bamboo and the creative expression of the artisans. In the hands-on learning corner, you can actually touch bamboo and experience the depth of bamboo crafts. In the popular bamboo bell making activity, you can create your own original work while being soothed by the gentle sound of bamboo. You can also learn traditional weaving methods and try your hand at making a beautiful flower basket in the slightly more difficult Shikainami flower basket making activity. You can take the completed work home as a souvenir of your trip. The attached shop & cafe sells a variety of bamboo products from Oita Prefecture. There is a selection of sophisticated items such as bamboo chopsticks, bamboo baskets, and bamboo accessories, which are popular as souvenirs. At the cafe's counter seats, you can enjoy a relaxing time while savoring fragrant coffee and local juices. Relax and rejuvenate in a calm space inspired by a bamboo grove, feeling the warmth of bamboo. It is an attractive space where you can feel the beauty and technology of Beppu bamboo crafts, and the natural power of bamboo with all five senses.
