  content: >-
    This work portrays the storefront of Hoteiya, with its large signboard and
    shop curtain, where an elegant lady—likely from a respectable household—is
    seen passing by. Hoteiya was a well-known drapery shop located in Owari-chō
    (present-day Ginza, Chuo Ward, Tokyo). The current Ginza 4-chome
    intersection was, in the Edo period, an area lined with long-established
    kimono merchants. Rendered in a powerful, large-format composition, the
    painting skillfully depicts the ornate and delicate patterns of the kimono,
    the slender and graceful posture of the beauty, and the detailed expressions
    of her face and hairstyle. The carefully executed depiction of the large
    storefront in the background vividly conveys the lively atmosphere of urban
    life.
