  content: >-
    Among the many variations of standing beauties that Shinryo excelled at, one
    of the more frequently depicted themes is that of a beauty with a child. In
    this work, the figure appears to be a refined woman of a wealthy household,
    dressed in a deep blue kimono boldly decorated with cherry blossoms and a
    vivid red obi with a large chrysanthemum pattern, accompanied by her young
    child. The depiction of the garments features swift, slightly textured brush
    lines for the drapery, while the bold cherry blossom design is rendered
    lightly using pale colors and diluted ink, creating a graceful and airy
    expression.
