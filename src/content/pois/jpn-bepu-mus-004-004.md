  title: Bamboo Light Bulb
  hero: 
  script: 
  content: >-
    This is a light bulb that reproduces the "bamboo filament" developed by the inventor Thomas Edison. It was reproduced by a Japanese engineer, and it lights up when you press the green button. The glowing part in the center is the bamboo filament. It is very thin, about 0.3 mm thick, and is made by carbonizing it in an electric furnace.
    
    Edison manufactured filaments using bamboo from Kyoto and succeeded in lighting them for a long time. Later, tungsten filaments were developed, and bamboo filaments disappeared in a short period of time, but it is a noteworthy fact that Japanese bamboo contributed to the history of light bulbs.
