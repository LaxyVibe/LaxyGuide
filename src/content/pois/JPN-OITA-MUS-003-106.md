    A stirrup (*abumi*) is a type of riding equipment used to support the
    rider’s foot while mounted. The front of this example is decorated with
    gold-inlaid figures of “Westerners” (Namban people). Images of Namban ships
    and foreigners served not only as exotic motifs, but were also associated
    with auspicious meanings—such as treasure ships bringing wealth and foreign
    deities of good fortune. The tread section is coated in vermilion lacquer,
    which has been worn down by friction, indicating that the stirrups were
    actually used.
