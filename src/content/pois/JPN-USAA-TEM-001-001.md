  content: |-
    Hishigata Pond is located within the grounds of Usa Jingu Shrine and is known as one of the "Three Famous Sawa Ponds of Japan" together with Sarusawa Pond in Nara Prefecture and Hirosawa Pond in Kyoto Prefecture.

    This pond is home to approximately 10,000 stalks of ancient lotus (revived from seeds recovered from a 2,000-year-old geological layer and transplanted in 1973). During blooming season, large pale-pink blossoms color the water's surface.

    The pond is also situated along the approach to the shrine buildings, and its tranquil atmosphere harmonized with greenery and the sound of flowing water is regarded as a place to calm both body and mind before worship.
