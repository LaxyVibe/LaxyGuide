Sengai's brushwork demonstrates the principle of \"one stroke, one breath\" - the circle was painted in a single, fluid motion without hesitation or correction. The incomplete nature of the circle is intentional, symbolizing that perfection is found in imperfection, and that the universe is in constant flux.
