  content: >-
    This long sword exceeds one meter in blade length, featuring a deep
    curvature and a magnificent, imposing form. The hamon is a straight temper
    line (suguha) with small irregularities. The blade is carved: on the *omote*
    (the outward-facing side when worn with the edge down), motifs such as a
    canopy, seed-syllable, lotus pedestal, *kuwagata* (helmet crest), and a
    sword with three-pronged hilt are engraved; on the *ura*, a seed-syllable,
    lotus pedestal, and plain sword are depicted. The inscription bears the
    surname “Minamoto,” and characteristics such as the beautiful jihada suggest
    the possibility that this work was forged by a Yamashiro-school swordsmith.
