  content: >-
    This painting portrays a beauty dressed in a chrysanthemum-patterned kimono,
    adorned with a chrysanthemum hairpin. She holds a fan in her right hand and
    lightly lifts the hem of her kimono with her left as she turns back
    gracefully—an elegant figure of a geisha. The facial features are rendered
    with extremely fine lines, especially the hairline, stray hairs, eyebrows,
    and eyelashes, where delicate brushwork can be clearly observed. Her lower
    lip is painted green, a style known as “sasabeni,” a type of lipstick
    popular in the late Edo period. This cosmetic, made from red pigment
    extracted from safflower, produced an iridescent sheen when repeatedly
    applied to the lower lip. The meticulous facial depiction combined with the
    light, refined rendering of the garments brings out the strong presence of
    the beauty.
