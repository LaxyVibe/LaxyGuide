  content: >-
    Takahashi Sohei was born into a merchant family in Kitsuki, Bungo Province.
    In 1822 (Bunsei 5), he became a pupil of Tanomura Chikuden when the latter
    visited Kitsuki. These fusuma paintings were created around 1830 (Tempo 1)
    when Sohei temporarily returned home from Osaka. Following the progression
    of the seasons from spring onward, he arranged over a dozen varieties of
    flowers—including magnolia, peony, hydrangea, hibiscus, chrysanthemum, plum,
    and narcissus—within the constrained format of the sliding-door panels. The
    inventive compositions, flowing ink lines, and clear, luminous coloring
    create a refined and serene visual atmosphere.
