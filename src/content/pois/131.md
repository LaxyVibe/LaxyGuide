  content: >-
    In the center of the composition, beneath a tree, a figure enjoys a boat
    outing. Beyond a great arch-shaped bend in the rocky cliffs, another figure
    is shown living in seclusion, backed by dense forest. On the left side of
    the painting, Tanomura Chikuden has signed the work and noted that “the
    green of the ravine’s trees grows luxuriantly, and it remains cool even in
    the afternoon.” At the top, Hirose Tanso of Hita has added a five-character
    quatrain. Tanso’s poem describes an aged oak under which flowers bloom
    quietly, their shadows cast upon the eaves’ pillar. He also mentions hearing
    the cry of a mountain bird, evoking flowers and sounds not actually depicted
    in the painting. Tanso’s poetic imagery enriches Chikuden’s idealized
    landscape, creating a deeply resonant scene where poem and painting
    harmonize.
