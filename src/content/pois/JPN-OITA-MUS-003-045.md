  content: >-
    This painting depicts the well-known Zen Buddhist story of Huike, who
    inherited the Dharma from Bodhidharma, the first patriarch of Zen. When
    Huike sought to become Bodhidharma’s disciple, Bodhidharma—absorbed in zazen
    while facing a wall—gave him no reply. To demonstrate his unwavering
    resolve, Huike cut off his own arm, and only then was he accepted. In the
    composition, beneath Bodhidharma who glares from a round window, Huike is
    shown with a sword, moments before severing his arm. The image is simplified
    yet powerful, with a uniquely comic-like arrangement.
