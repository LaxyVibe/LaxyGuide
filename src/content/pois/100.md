  content: >-
    This piece is a *seigan*, a portable Christian altar used to house a holy
    image. The front of the double-opening doors is decorated with
    chrysanthemums and morning glories, while the reverse bears designs of
    grapes and weeping cherry blossoms, all executed in *makie* and
    mother-of-pearl inlay. The painting shows a saint in the center with arms
    outstretched and gazing upward, flanked by two angels—one on the left
    opening a book, and one on the right holding a lily-like flower. The saint
    has been suggested to be either Saint Francis of Assisi receiving the
    stigmata or Saint Dominic, founder of the Rosary prayer. This altar was
    reportedly discovered in Puerto Rico, a port of call for Spanish ships.
