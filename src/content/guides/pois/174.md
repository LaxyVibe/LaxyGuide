  content: >-
    Among the seven flowers of autumn, pampas grass and bush clover are
    prominently depicted at the center, while maidenflower, bellflower, and
    pinks are arranged in the lower portion. Instead of using malachite green,
    the leaves of the central bush clover—serving as the main motif—are rendered
    in light ink with the *mokkotsu* (boneless) technique. This ink-based
    expression lends the work a calm, refined autumn atmosphere. The lightly
    reddening leaves near the base of the bush clover, the elegant pale pink of
    the pinks, the gold-mud lines indicating the central veins of the
    bush-clover leaves, and the sprinkled gold flakes throughout the composition
    all demonstrate Shuei’s excellent sense of color and balance in using
    diverse pigments. Paintings of autumn grasses were a favored theme of the
    Rinpa school, represented by Kyoto masters such as Tawaraya Sōtatsu and
    Ogata Kōrin, and later carried on by Edo-Rinpa artists including Sakai
    Hōitsu (1761–1829) and his pupil Ikeda Koson (1803–68). Shuei, said to have
    studied under Ikeda Koson, inherited the Edo-Rinpa qualities of delicate
    coloration and poetic, lyrical design.

    금니로 그린 선, 화면 곳곳에 흩뿌린 금모래 장식 등은 다양한 안료를 균형 있게 사용한 뛰어난 색채 감각을 보여준다. 추초도는 교토의
    소타쓰와 고린 등 린파 화파가 즐겨 그린 주제로, 에도 린파의 사카이 호이츠(1761–1829)와 제자 이케다
    고손(1803–1868)에게도 계승되었다. 이케다 고손에게 배웠다고 전하는 슈에이는 에도 린파 특유의 섬세한 색채감과 시정이 깃든
    조형성을 이어받고 있다.
