  content: >-
    Ashikaga Takauji planned the establishment of Ankokuji temples across the
    provinces. Among them, the Ankokuji in Bungo Province is said to have been
    founded during the Ōei era (1394–1426) by the Rinzai Zen monk Zekkai Chūshin
    (1336–1405) of the Musō lineage. This statue was originally enshrined at
    Jizō-ji Temple in Yamashina, Kyoto, and was transferred to Ankokuji in 1896.
    It depicts Takauji in formal court robes (ikan sokutai) at life size. Viewed
    from the front, it forms a symmetrical, upright triangular composition; from
    the side, it shows notable depth. Created close to the period of Takauji’s
    lifetime, it is regarded as a valuable sculpture conveying his actual
    appearance.

    무소파의 선승 절해중신(1336–1405)이 창건한 것으로 전해진다. 이 상은 원래 교토 야마시나의 지조지에 봉안되어 있었으며,

    측면에서는 두께감이 두드러진다. 다카우지 생존 시기에 가까운 시기에 제작된 것으로 여겨지며, 그의 생전 용모를 전하는 귀중한 조각상이다.
